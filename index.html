07 / Case Study

LIFE

A film made with limited resources, countless nights and a team that believed in the story.

The Story

Life is a light-hearted story of an unemployed B.Tech graduate and his middle-class family. A heartfelt journey of love, expectations, and family bonds.

The Idea

Life is based on the lives of unemployed graduates in a middle-class family.

The Making

This was the team's first proper short film. Lighting and filmmaking terminology were new to the team. The film was shot across almost 20 nights, usually from 7 PM to 11 PM and sometimes until 12 AM. Total cost was around ₹2,000.

Direction

Soumith Yeruva — Writer & Director. His cool, cooperative approach and coordination helped the team. Despite confusion around a few scenes, he worked through the challenges and completed the film.

Cinematography

Sreevanth Konatham & Rakesh Vaddi · Drone — Tarak. The entire film was shot at night and around 90% was indoors, making lighting a key challenge with limited equipment and resources.

Editing

Sreevanth Konatham — Editor. Editing was a tough part of LIFE. The approximately 20-minute film was divided into four sections, roughly 25% each. Post-production took around one and a half months.

Music

Chandu Punyamanthula — Music Composer & Producer. He worked for approximately one month without charging the production, contributing his time and passion to the project.
